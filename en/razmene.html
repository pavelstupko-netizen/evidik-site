Fiscal cash registers: ESIR and L-PFR

In Serbia every cash-register receipt goes through fiscalisation. ESIR is the program that issues the receipt. The fiscal receipt processor signs the receipt and sends it to the Tax Administration. A local L-PFR works offline, and a virtual V-PFR runs on the Tax Administration server.

ESIR→L-PFR / V-PFR→Poreska uprava→Evidik

Evidik will take fiscal receipts from the register and post sales, VAT and cash. A small helper program connects the browser with the register and the printer.
